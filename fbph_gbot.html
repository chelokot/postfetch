Допис користувача Alex Krakovsky

Alex Krakovsky разом з Дмитром Лубінцем.

5 дн.  ·
I want to publicly thank the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights Dmytro Lubinets and his wonderful team for saving lives, particularly the life of my cousin.
https://www.facebook.com/dmytro.lubinets
The last few weeks were not easy for me.
On May 27, my cousin, the father of two who was taking care of his 80 years old mother, was brutally beaten on the street and taken to the Territorial Center of Recruitment. Once arrived, doctors diagnosed multiple broken rib bones, broken fingers and broken hand. Doctors refused to sign a medical conclusion that my cousin is healthy and ready to be a soldier.
They had let him go.
But two days later he was caught again next to his house. And they didn't let him go. In spite of his urgent medical condition.
He spent nearly 20 (!) days locked, isolated, without any connection. They didn't allow an attorney to come in and help. Doctors constantly refused to sign papers, they stated he has multiple broken bones. However no medical treatment was provided.
He lives with an 80 years old mother that struggles with diabet. She was left completely alone without any help.
The Police ignored our calls for help.
My cousin and other hostages like him were released yesterday. I had tears in my eyes.
Thank you Dmytro Lubinets and your team. There are still professional and honest people in this world!
Obviously, we all support the people of Ukraine in this terrible war, There's no excuse for this horrible aggression. However, there shouldn't be such terrible treatment to ordinary people. It is unacceptable.
Thanks to all the people involved in this. Justice finally prevailed.
Дмитро Лубінець
